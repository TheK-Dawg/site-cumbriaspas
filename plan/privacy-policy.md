# Privacy Policy

Cumbria Spas Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Cumbria Spas collects, uses, and protects your personal information. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, and details about your hot tub when you contact us, schedule an appointment, or use our website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide you with the hot tub repair services you request, to communicate with you about your service, and to improve our services.

4. Information Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties, except as required by law or with your consent.

5. Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, and delete your personal information. Please contact us if you wish to exercise these rights.

7. Contact Us

For any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@cumbriaspas.co.uk.
