## Why so many hot tubs need repair now

Hot tub sales boomed during lockdown, and many of those tubs were never serviced. Six or seven years on, a lot of them are leaking and in a bit of a state. David, who owns Cumbria Spas and does the repairs himself, has been busier with servicing this year than for about four years.

If yours was bought around then and has not been looked at since, a full service is the place to start, and any repair it needs follows from there.

## What a full hot tub service involves

A full service has nine steps and takes up to three hours:

1. The old water stays in to start with.
2. A hot tub flush is added and the jets run on full power for 45 minutes, to clean the pipes and bacteria from the water lines.
3. The tub is emptied and the footwell vacuumed.
4. Clean water is sprayed over the seats and vacuumed again.
5. Every jet is vacuumed to clear the old water from the lines.
6. The shell, the lid and the steps are cleaned.
7. The filters are inspected and cleaned. If the old ones are not fit to go back, new filters are charged for.
8. The tub is refilled.
9. The water is chemically balanced, ready to use.

The flush in step two is what cleans the bacteria out of the water lines, so it comes before the tub is drained. Year-round maintenance packages are also offered: ask for the details.

## Chemical balancing and automated water care

Sorting out the chemicals is one of the main pains of owning a hot tub, and people often ask for a domestic tub that does it automatically. Standard domestic hot tubs do not come with an automatic chemical system: they need manual chemical balancing.

There are two routes to automated water care:

- **A commercial spa with a plant room.** Automated pH balancing and chemical dosing come with a commercial spa and its plant room, and that can still be sold to a homeowner who wants it.
- **Arctic Spas' Spa Boy.** The one domestic route is the Spa Boy saltwater system on an Arctic Spa. Medical-grade sensors continuously analyse the water, sanitisation adjusts automatically, and pH alerts reach the control panel and the app, with a Boost button. Cumbria Spas is an [Arctic Spas dealer](/arctic-spas-dealer/).

For everyone else, Cumbria Spas sells [hot tub chemicals](/chemicals/) in small quantities, down to a single pack, and ships them to you.

## Sand or glass: hot tub and spa filtration

Many systems, particularly commercial spas with plant rooms, still use sand filters, where the sand is changed about every two years. Cumbria Spas fits glass (pearl glass) filtration instead, changed about every ten years. It gives better water quality and needs roughly half the backflushing, which saves time and water.

The filters inside a domestic tub are the ones checked at every service (step seven above). The Kubilų pasaulis wood-fired range uses a sand filter with a UV lamp.
