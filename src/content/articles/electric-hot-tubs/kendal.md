An electric hot tub is the convenient choice: no stove to light, and the tub kept at temperature. ## Electric or wood-fired: the same tub shapes

Cumbria Spas works in partnership with Kubilų pasaulis, the largest manufacturer in Lithuania of premium, luxury and custom hot tubs. It makes the electric tubs, the [wood-fired hot tubs](/wood-fired-hot-tubs/) and the saunas, and the same tub shapes come in electric and wood-fired versions.

That makes the choice a straight one. You are not picking between two different tubs, only between two ways of heating the same one:

- **Electric** is about convenience. The heater keeps the water at temperature, so the tub is ready when you are.
- **Wood-fired** is about speed and fuel. A stove heats the water far faster, and it runs on your own wood.

## Heat-up time and the 40°C governor

From cold, an electric hot tub takes 14 to 16 hours to heat. A wood-fired tub takes about 2 to 4 hours to reach around 38°C, and a 2-metre family tub takes about two hours even in hard winter.

Standard electric tubs are governed to 40°C. For most people that is plenty: body temperature is 36.7°C, and most people like the water at about 38°C in autumn and about 40°C in hard winter. If you want more control than that, a wood-fired tub lets you choose.
